Privacy Policy

This policy describes the privacy practices of University Exam Coach V3.0.0, a skills-based assistant used through ChatGPT.

1. How the service works

The plugin operates through ChatGPT. It does not operate its own website login, student account system, application server, analytics service or database, and the developer does not receive chat content through a separate plugin backend.

2. ChatGPT and information you provide

When you use the coach, information you choose to enter or upload—such as prompts, syllabus documents, notes and study answers—is processed within ChatGPT. The handling, storage, retention, use and deletion of ChatGPT conversations and uploaded files are governed by OpenAI's applicable terms, privacy policy and product settings. The developer of this plugin does not control those platform practices. Review OpenAI's privacy information and ChatGPT data controls before sharing information.

3. Minimise personal information

Do not provide passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, confidential university records or other sensitive personal information unless you have a clear need and are comfortable with the platform's applicable policies. Use anonymised examples where possible.

4. Email support

If you contact the developer at abhishekthakur11@shoolini.online, the information in your email will be received in that mailbox and used to respond to your request. Avoid including sensitive information. Email may be retained in the mailbox according to the account provider's settings and the developer's ordinary mailbox practices.

5. Sharing and third parties

The plugin has no separate backend through which the developer collects or sells student chat data. ChatGPT platform processing and any services you independently choose to use are subject to their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Children's privacy

This study tool is not designed to solicit personal information from children under 13. Do not submit a child's personal information.

7. Policy changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. For questions about this plugin policy, contact abhishekthakur11@shoolini.online. For data handled by ChatGPT, use OpenAI's official privacy and support channels.
